Emergency Safety Protocols

Erai Dam Disaster Management Guidelines and Safety thresholds.

LEVEL 1 - NOMINAL

Reservoir capacity is below 85% warning threshold. Basin inflow is normal. Gate G1 & G2 on automatic standby mode. Downstream flood alert cleared.

LEVEL 2 - WARNING

Capacity reaches or exceeds 85% (192.5M m³). Early proactive release initiated to prevent storage overflow. G1 & G2 opened proportionally. Channel flows monitored hourly.

LEVEL 3 - CRITICAL

Precipitation results in projected volume exceeding warning limits and discharge exceeds downstream safe capacity (800 m³/s). Gates G3 & G4 unlocked. Sirens triggered in Padmapur and Datala.
